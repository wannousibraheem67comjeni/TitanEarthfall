# Google Play listing — English (en-US)

**App name (max 30):** Hopinet: 3D Runner Adventure

**Short description (max 80):** Run, swim, jump & fly through 6 worlds. Ride boats, cars and planes!

**Full description (max 4000):**

Hop. Swim. Soar. Hopinet is a 3D endless runner where the road never stays the same.

🏃 RUN, JUMP, SLIDE — and now SWIM and DIVE
Sprint down sunny harbours, neon bays, tropical lagoons, emerald jungles, frozen fjords and golden dunes. When the road ends in the sea, dive in and swim through it; when a bridge is broken, jump the gap.

🚤 RIDE EVERYTHING
Hop into a race car, a speed boat, a giant ship or a plane you find on the road. Smash through obstacles, chase gold in the sky, fly through rings and dodge storms.

🧲 POWER UP
Magnet, Gold Doubler, Jet Pack, Bomb Magnet and the Helmet (the sound muffles while you wear it!). Upgrade every one of them.

🗺️ 36 STAGES ON A REAL WORLD MAP
Play a campaign across the real map: Lisbon, Tokyo Bay, the Maldives, the Amazon, the Norwegian fjords and the Dubai desert. Earn 3 stars per stage. Optionally pin your own location on the map (it never leaves your device).

🏠 BUILD A LUXURY HOME
Build your dream house in every world, then add a pool, fountain, supercar, helipad, yacht and a golden statue. Every luxury item boosts your coins.

🐰 COLLECT 7 HEROES
Hopi the rabbit, Kiko the fox, Bruno the bear, Mimi the cat, Ziggy the frog, Olo the owl and Rex the dino — each with outfits and a special perk.

🏴‍☠️ BEAT CAPTAIN KRAB
The pirate crab raids the sky in his airship. Catch his bombs and bring it down!

🎵 Exciting original music for every world. Plays fully offline. No ads.

In-app purchases (Google Play edition) are optional; everything can also be earned by playing.
