Hopinet — Privacy Policy

App: Hopinet (com.elevo.hopinet, com.elevo.hopinet.free) · Developer: Elevo · العربية

The short version

Hopinet is an offline game. It has no accounts, no game server, no database, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

What is stored on your device

  • Your game progress (coins, gems, unlocked characters, houses, settings, high scores) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage and in the web view's local storage. If Android Auto Backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this save file in your own Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device. We cannot access that backup.
  • Purchase records (Google Play edition only): the product IDs and anonymous purchase tokens that Google Play returns, kept locally so a purchase is never granted twice.

Optional: approximate location

The in-game world map has an optional My location button. If you press it and allow the permission, the game asks Android for your approximate location once, rounds it to about 10 km, and uses it only to show a pin on the map and the nearest game world. The rounded value is kept only on your device and is never sent anywhere. You can remove it at any time with Forget my location or by revoking the permission in Android settings. The game works fully without it.

Purchases (Google Play edition)

Real-money purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. The game never sees your payment method or card number. Google's own privacy policy applies to purchases: policies.google.com/privacy. The free edition (com.elevo.hopinet.free) contains no purchase code and no billing permission.

Network access

The game does not need the internet to play. The Android app declares the INTERNET permission only because the Android web view and Google Play Billing (Google Play edition) require it; the game itself does not contact any server of ours.

Children

Hopinet does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. Questions: please open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
